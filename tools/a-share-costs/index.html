Formulas and conventions

commission = max(value × rate, minimum) per order, rounded to 0.01 CNY regulatory = value × (handling + management) only when the commission is quoted net transfer = value × transfer rate both sides stamp duty = value × stamp rate sells only impact = value × impact bp / 10,000 both sides, your estimate round trip = buy costs + sell costs at the same value; bp = round trip / value × 10,000 annual drag = one-way turnover × round-trip cost as a share of the portfolio minimum binds when value < minimum / rate

“All-in” means the commission already includes the exchange handling fee and the CSRC management fee, as most brokers quote it; “Net + fees” adds them on top. Each fee is rounded to 0.01 CNY separately, as on a broker's contract note. One-way turnover of 8 means the portfolio is bought 8 times and sold 8 times a year. The annual drag uses the round-trip cost of the order amount entered, so a portfolio traded in small orders also shows the cost of the minimum commission. With a share price, the order amount is rounded down to whole lots: 100 shares on the main board and ChiNext; on the STAR Market at least 200 shares, then single shares.

What the numbers say

  • Stamp duty dominates at normal commission rates. At 2.5 bp commission, stamp duty is about half of a round trip; below 1 bp it is more than two thirds.
  • Small orders pay for the minimum. With a 5 CNY minimum and 2.5 bp, orders below 20,000 CNY pay a higher rate. Splitting a portfolio into many small orders raises costs even when the headline rate is low.
  • Turnover is the multiplier. A round trip of 10 bp is small; eight round trips a year is 0.8% of the portfolio every year, before market impact, which for small caps is often larger than all the fees together.
  • Backtests need the schedule by date. Before 28 August 2023 every sale paid 5 bp more stamp duty, and before 29 April 2022 both sides paid another 0.1 bp transfer fee.

FAQ

What does it cost to buy and sell an A-share stock?
At the fee schedule in force since 28 August 2023, a round trip of 100,000 CNY with a commission of 2.5 basis points (including exchange fees) and a 5 CNY minimum costs about 102 CNY: 25 CNY commission on each side, 1 CNY transfer fee on each side and 50 CNY stamp duty on the sale. That is 10.2 basis points before any market impact.
Who pays stamp duty?
Only the seller. It has been 0.05% of the traded value since 28 August 2023 and was 0.1% before that, back to September 2008.
Is the exchange handling fee included in my commission?
Usually yes. Most brokers quote an all-in commission that already contains the exchange handling fee (0.00341%) and the CSRC management fee (0.002%), together 0.00541%. Some institutional and low-cost accounts quote a net commission and charge these fees on top; choose that option if your contract notes list them separately.
Why are small orders so expensive?
Because of the minimum commission. With a 5 CNY minimum and a 2.5 basis point rate, every order below 20,000 CNY pays the minimum, so a 5,000 CNY order pays 10 basis points of commission on each side instead of 2.5.
Which fee schedule should a backtest use?
The one in force on each trade date. A backtest that applies today's 0.05% stamp duty to trades before 28 August 2023 understates the cost of every sale by 5 basis points.
Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything is computed in your browser.
