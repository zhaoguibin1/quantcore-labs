PRIVACY

Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies set by this site, and nothing you type into a calculator is sent anywhere.

Your inputs

All calculations run in your browser with JavaScript. Prices, equity curves, option chains and other inputs you enter or paste are not uploaded or stored by this site.

Requests the pages make

  • quantcore-labs.com serves the pages and /data/rates.json (the Treasury curve). No personal data is included in these requests.
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net is contacted only when you click "3D view" on the implied-volatility page, to load the Plotly charting library.
  • cdn.tailwindcss.com is contacted by the efficient-frontier and Monte Carlo pages to load their styling.

Like any website, these servers receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver files.

Hosting and statistics

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. Aggregate visit statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track individuals across sites.

Changes

If the site ever adds features that store data, such as accounts, this page will be updated before they go live.
