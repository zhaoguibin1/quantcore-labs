# Markowitz Efficient Frontier Calculator

> Client-side mean-variance tool for three assets: plots random long-only portfolios in risk-return space and reports the highest-Sharpe portfolio found.
> URL: https://quantcore-labs.com/tools/portfolio-efficient-frontier/

## Inputs

- Expected annual return and volatility for three assets (for example equities, bonds, alternatives)
- Risk-free rate R_f (%)
- Number of random portfolios to sample

## Method

```
R_p     = sum_i w_i * R_i
sigma_p = sqrt( w' * Sigma * w ),   Sigma_ij = sigma_i * sigma_j * rho_ij
Sharpe  = (R_p - R_f) / sigma_p
Weights: w_i >= 0, sum_i w_i = 1   (long-only, fully invested)
```

- The correlation between every pair of assets is fixed at rho = 0.3. It is not an input.
- Portfolios are generated by drawing three uniform numbers and normalising them to sum to 1. This samples the weight simplex non-uniformly: it concentrates around equal weights and under-samples corners.
- The reported "maximum Sharpe" portfolio is the best one among the sampled portfolios. It approximates the true tangency portfolio, and gets closer as the number of samples grows.

## Outputs

- Scatter of sampled portfolios (volatility vs expected return)
- Best-Sharpe portfolio: Sharpe ratio, expected return, volatility and weights

## Notes

- Diversification lowers risk whenever assets are less than perfectly correlated (rho < 1).
- Mean-variance results are very sensitive to expected-return inputs. Small changes can swing the weights.
- Educational and analytical use only; not investment advice.
